Architecture

Single-node reliability without distributed queue sprawl

No Kafka clusters, Redis brokers, or SQS dead-letter race conditions. One self-contained proxy backed by crash-safe SQLite WAL commits.

Keep-Alive Ingress (<10ms)

Returns an instant 200 OK once durable WAL synchronous=FULL fsync succeeds, while pooling persistent HTTP/HTTPS sockets downstream.

~96h Delivery Budget (10 Attempts)

Outlasts Stripe's 3-day retry schedule with jittered backoff (30s to 48h), plus instant local startup crash recovery and claim leases.

Expiration-Defeating Re-Signing

Re-signs outbound Stripe and Svix timestamps at t=now and supports up to 5,000-event CLI or UI replays with forensic provenance headers.

AES-256-GCM & Compliance Mode

Encrypts signing secrets, headers, and bodies at rest. Enable HOOKARMOR_REQUIRE_SECRETS to forbid unverified endpoints plus full IPv4/IPv6 SSRF blocking.

Sentinel & Rejection Alerts

Tracks failing streaks and ingress rejections (rotated secrets or scheme mismatches) with throttled Slack/Discord webhooks and Prometheus /metrics.

Online Backups & Dev Tunneling

Stream zero-downtime SQLite snapshots with hookarmor backup or tunnel live signed payloads straight into localhost:3000 in one terminal command.

Integration

Drop-in compatibility or isolated internal verification
