Download Free on GitHub How HookArmor Works

Built for Developers Tired of Silent Revenue Loss

A drop-in proxy that protects your business from the harsh realities of production infrastructure.

⚡

Fast Ingress Buffer (<15ms)

Returns an instant 200 OK to Stripe, Shopify, or Clerk immediately upon receipt. Payment gateways never enter backoff retry purgatory or mark endpoints dead.

💀

Durable Dead-Letter Queue

If your backend responds 500, 502, 504, 429, or times out, the exact raw body and headers are safely quarantined in SQLite with complete error diagnostic traces.

🔄

1-Click Bulk Replay

Deploy your fix, then click "Replay All Dead-Letter" in the dashboard or run node bin/hookarmor.js replay --failed to restore customer subscriptions in 2 seconds.

🔐

HMAC Signature Passthrough

Preserves exact raw bytes and cryptographic signature headers (stripe-signature, x-shopify-hmac-sha256) so downstream security checks never break.

🚨

Discord & Slack Webhooks

Get notified within 1 second of an endpoint failure with the event ID, HTTP status code, and error body delivered directly to your engineering channel.

💻

Zero-Friction Local Tunnel

Run node bin/hookarmor.js listen http://localhost:3000/api/webhook to test production events against your local IDE with hot reloading and zero ngrok hassles.
